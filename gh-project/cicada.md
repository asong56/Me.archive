# Why I made Cicada

I made cicada because I found that most of the whiteboards need account and is online,
so I made one that is offline, no account, you can share your drawings with url, and it is only one single html.

---

It feels closer to opening a blank paper than opening a software.
Its name matches this project, cicada.
A cicada appears, makes a sound, and disappears.
This project is similar, create something quickly, share it, move on, or close it.

---

The first version proves that the idea works, but when I move on to the second version, there was a lot of problems.
I added export to png, and automatically save, but that makes Cicada seems to be harder to use.
So maybe I should not improve the experience, because the original experience is very good.

---

Maybe I should add functions instead.
I might add adding images, shapes, all serves for one idea, which is drawing.
A whiteboard which cannot draw smoothly is not a good whiteboard.
The question is "how do people discover a tiny tool at the exact moment they need it?", and Cicada aims to answer it.
